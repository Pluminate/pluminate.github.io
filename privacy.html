Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers Pluminate Free, Pluminate Lite (the Mac App Store edition), PlumTerm and this website. The developer is Landoulsi Technologies FZE LLC.

Summary

Pluminate collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting. Your code stays on your computer. The only time Pluminate sends anything over the network is when you use a feature that needs it, and those features are listed below.

Files and folders

Pluminate reads and writes the folders and files you open. Pluminate Lite runs in the macOS App Sandbox and can only reach folders you choose through the system open panel. Your code and documents are never transmitted to us.

Settings and recent folders

Your settings and your list of recent folders are stored locally on your computer and are not shared.

Pluminate Free: features that use the network

Pluminate Lite has no network features. Pluminate Free has the following, and each works only when you use it:

  • AI commit messages. If you add your own Gemini API key in Settings and ask for a commit message, Pluminate sends the changes you are committing to Google's Gemini API with that key. Google handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent if you do not add a key or do not use the feature. We never receive it. Your key is stored on your computer.
  • Embedded browser. When you open a web page inside Pluminate, the page loads from the site you chose, like in any browser. That site may see your IP address and set cookies.
  • Import from Chrome. If you ask Pluminate to import web data from your local Chrome profile, it is read on your computer only and is not sent to us.
  • Git, terminal and tools you run. Commands you run, including Git operations against remote servers, are carried out by you on your own machine and talk to the hosts you choose.

PlumTerm

PlumTerm collects no analytics, usage data or crash reports, and does not send your commands, files or terminal output to us. If this ever changes, it will be optional and described here first. Some optional features contact third-party services at your request, such as AI commands and pull request lookups through the GitHub CLI. Those services have their own privacy policies.

This website and downloads

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics or cookies. Its fonts are served from the site itself, not from a third party. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the download files are served from GitHub Releases. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses to serve pages and files, under GitHub's privacy statement. The theme you pick is saved in your browser only.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you, keep it only as long as needed to do so and to meet our legal and record-keeping obligations, and never sell it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing privacy@pluminate.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If we add a feature that collects data, we will describe it here before it ships.
