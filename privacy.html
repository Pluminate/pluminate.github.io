Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers Pluminate Lite, the Mac App Store edition of Pluminate, and this website. The developer is Landoulsi Technologies FZE LLC.

Summary

Pluminate Lite collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads, and it sends no user data over the network.

Files and folders

Pluminate Lite runs in the macOS App Sandbox. It only reads and writes the folders and files you choose to open through the system open panel. Your code and documents stay on your Mac and are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Settings and recent folders

Your settings and list of recent folders are stored locally on your Mac and are not shared.

This website

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as IP addresses to serve the pages.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you, keep it only as long as needed, and never sell it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing privacy@pluminate.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
