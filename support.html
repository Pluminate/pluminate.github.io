Support

Help for Pluminate Free (the direct download for macOS, Windows and Linux) and Pluminate Lite (the Mac App Store edition). Email us and we will get back to you.

Email: support@pluminate.com

Reply time: we usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I open a folder?

Choose File › Open Folder… and select a folder. In Pluminate Lite, which runs in the macOS App Sandbox, the app can only read and write folders you open this way. If a file or folder will not open, open its parent folder from the same menu.

Which edition do I have?

Pluminate Lite comes from the Mac App Store and is sandboxed. Pluminate Free is the direct download from the download page and includes Git, a terminal and code navigation. The name is shown in the About window. Compare the two editions.

Which Mac download do I need?

Choose Apple menu › About This Mac. If the chip is Apple M1 or later, download the Apple silicon version. If it says Intel, download the Intel version.

Which Linux systems are supported?

Debian 13 and Ubuntu 25.04 or later, on 64-bit Intel or AMD (amd64) and 64-bit ARM (arm64). Older releases such as Ubuntu 24.04 LTS ship a version of Qt that is too old. Install with sudo apt install ./pluminate_amd64.deb.

Where are my settings stored?

On your computer only. Pluminate Lite keeps them inside its sandbox container, and Pluminate Free keeps them in your user account's standard application settings location. Nothing is sent to us. Open Settings from the Pluminate menu to change them.

Why can't Pluminate Lite see my other files?

This is by design. The App Sandbox limits Pluminate Lite to the folders you choose, which keeps the rest of your Mac private.

Does Pluminate collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics or tracking in either edition. Pluminate Free has one optional feature that contacts an outside service when you use it. See the privacy policy.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@pluminate.com and include:

  • your operating system and version (on a Mac: Apple menu › About This Mac)
  • the Pluminate version and edition (Pluminate › About)
  • the steps that lead to the problem, what you expected and what happened
  • a screenshot, if it helps
Can I request a language or a feature?

Yes, please do. Email us at support@pluminate.com. Support for more languages, a faster IDE and AI features are our current priorities, and requests help us choose what comes first.
